# 30 Days to Ascend — Pilot (1:10) — Treatment

Style: 1930s Rubber Hose Cartoon (greyscale, 4:3 gate, hot-jazz band). Language: English. Captions/voice: period title cards, no voiceover (voice model unavailable offline).

## Three candidate structures
A. **Countdown calendar** — day counter flips 30→29 across five gag stations; ends on Day 2 teaser.
B. **Talent show** — the hero auditions face-parts on a stage, a judge rates each.
C. **Singalong** — bouncing ball over a "looksmaxxing anthem", set acts out each lyric.
**Choice: C+A hybrid** — bouncing-ball singalong lyrics ARE the caption cards; the HUD counter ticks as stations pass. The singalong gives sound-first rhythm and makes every joke a sung line.

## Logline
Paul, a lumpy potato in a bow tie, has 30 days before the group-chat Tier List drops ("Rat Tier" awaits). He tries mewing, jaw-trainers, squinty hunter eyes and platform shoes — and the PSL meter doesn't move. Viewers vote for Day 2.

## Arc
Setup (0–10s) title + deadline text → Turn (10–50s) four stations, each a gag, meter stuck at 0.0 → End (50–70s) ironic hope, vote card MEW vs MOG, "DAY 2 STARTS" iris-out.

## Benchmark
Learn: early-sound-era "whole world dances on the beat", bouncing-ball cards. Don't take: any existing character, designs, melodies.

## Beats (seconds)
0–4 title "30 DAYS TO ASCEND" (Shrikhand), HUD: DAYS LEFT 30 / PSL 0.0
4–10 Paul, text: the group chat tier list drops in 30 days — he is "Rat Tier"
10–20 Station 1 MIRROR: rates himself, mirror shatters ("3 out of 10 — it's the mirror's fault")
20–30 Station 2 MEWING: tongue on roof of mouth, doesn't speak for 3 days, silence gag (band stop)
30–40 Station 3 JAWLINE: jaw-trainer ball chewed, jaw "pops" into a hinge; still soft
40–50 Station 4 HEIGHTMAXXING: platform shoes, wobbles, falls; "mogged by a door frame"
50–60 Meter check: PSL 0.0 → hope; "frame is everything"
60–70 vote card MEW vs MOG, "DAY 2 STARTS", iris out

## Sound
Tempo 120 bpm (12 frames/beat at 24 fps), synthesized stride piano / clarinet / tuba, woodblock, slide whistle; band stop on mewing. Optical-soundtrack ageing. −14 LUFS.

## Captions
Period title cards (IM Fell English SC), merged lines, ≥1.8 s holds.
