Most myths end with the hero winning. The Norse myths tell you almost from the start exactly how everybody dies.

Odin knows a wolf will swallow him whole. Thor knows he'll win his greatest fight and die nine steps later, and they fight anyway.

This is Ragnarök, a three-year winter, a wolf that eats the sun, a ship built from the fingernails of the dead, a battle that kills almost every god, and a fire that burns the world. And then the part people forget, the world comes back.

Here's exactly what happens in order.
